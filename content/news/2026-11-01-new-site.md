---
title: The new league site
date: 2026-11-01
---

Standings, team pages and records now come straight from ESPN and update every day during the season, so nobody has to type numbers in by hand anymore.

The all-time standings count every game since 2017: regular season, playoffs and consolation. They include this season's games as they finish. On a phone you can sort by wins, win %, points or point differential, search for a name, and hide retired managers.

To post news, add a Markdown file to `content/news` on GitHub.
