Although this season was controversial for trading, AJ prevailed with newly acquired Ceedee Lamb and Christian McCaffery pushing him through a 10 game win streak, a league record. This was AJ's message to the fantasy league,

"Thank you it's been good season thank you all for your competitive seasons looking forward to next seasons improved changes"

Last but not least, the fight for last place was a hard and close fought battle for the pancakes. Luke's Chubby Bunnies were just short of victory as they were close of escaping their losing record. Unfortunately, the Chubby Bunnies remained in their hole and awaiting the pancakes they had so desired as a punishment. In behalf of the league, we thank you Luke.
